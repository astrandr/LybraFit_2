Paste Insert Data from clipboard

To insert data from clip board select a row in the table and choose Data > Paste from the menu or click on the Paste/insert from clipboard button .

You can also use Ctrl+V keys.  The data must consists only of numeric entries. The numbers are split based on standard separators: space, return, coma, semicolons, colons, tabs.

The following three formats will produce the same result:

1          1.23

2          2.45

3          5.67

 

 

1          1.23    2          2.45    3          5.67

 

1          1.23    2         

2.45    3          5.67

 

1          1.23, 2;2.45:3

5.67
